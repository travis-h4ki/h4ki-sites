Child sponsorship

Be the one who shows up for a child.

When you sponsor a child through Hope 4 Kids International, you walk with them through school, health and everyday life. It starts with one monthly gift and can grow into a lifelong friendship.

One relationship. A lifetime of hope.

Sponsorship is where it starts. From there, supporters visit the communities we serve, join projects, come home as ambassadors and invite others in. Here's what that journey looks like.

01
Step one · Dignity

Sponsor a child

Children are waiting for a sponsor who will stand with them, whether for day school, boarding school or higher education. Your monthly gift equips a child to learn, grow and see a future with purpose.

  • Get to know one child by name and story
  • Support their education and wellbeing through H4KI programs
  • Stay connected as they grow
