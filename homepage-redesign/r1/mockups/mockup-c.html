02 · Our work

Four pillars. One purpose.

Hope 4 Kids International is a faith-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit committed to children living in poverty. Everything we do restores dignity, health, joy and love.

Dignity

Every child is known by name and equipped to become an educated, self-sufficient leader who can help break the cycle of poverty.

Child SponsorshipEducation

Health

Safe water, nutrition and medical care give children and families the foundation to thrive.

Water 4 Kids InternationalMedicalFood 4 Kids

Joy

Childhood is meant for learning, play and belonging. Supporters share in that joy when they visit and serve.

Mission TripsEvents

Love

We share the love of Jesus through presence, service and long-term relationships with the communities we serve.
