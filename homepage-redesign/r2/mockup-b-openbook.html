Who we are, in 20 seconds

Hope for children. Opportunity for communities.

Hope 4 Kids International is a faith-based ministry. Since 1973, we have partnered with local leaders to restore hope and create opportunity for children living in poverty, through sponsorship, education, medical care, clean water and food.

How the work actually happens

  1. Local teams lead

    H4KI's national staff in Uganda run our schools, the medical center and well maintenance.

  2. You choose where it goes

    Give where it's needed most, or to a specific program, school or project.

  3. You see the results

    Sponsors receive their child's photo and profile, and can write letters or visit on a team trip.
