The journey

After you say yes

Sponsorship is a relationship, not a transaction. Here is what the first year can look like.

  1. 1

    Meet your student

    You receive your student's photo and a profile of their community, school grade, health and hobbies.

  2. 2

    Stay in touch

    Write letters and share family photos. Hearing from you helps your student feel valued.

  3. 3

    Visit

    Visit your student by joining one of our team trips.

  4. 4

    Share

    Invite friends and family to sponsor too, as your first Ambassador mission.

One path, many years

Brian's path

Sponsorship opened the door, and boarding school changed everything. Brian had a safe place to learn, and teachers, local pastors, and a community that mentored and cared for him.

Brian was part of Fairmont's first graduating class, and today, at 25, he's thriving, having also earned a vocational certificate in a growing industry.
