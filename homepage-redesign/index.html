Decision open as of November 4, 2026

Independent review

How the scores moved

A separate reviewer with no access to the build conversation scored each direction, the team made changes, and the reviewer scored again. Round 1 used 7 criteria (total of 35); round 2 used 8 criteria tied to the board brief (total of 40), so totals do not compare across rounds. 5 means excellent, 3 workable with changes, 1 does not meet the need.

Round 1 to round 2

What carried forward, what changed

Carried forward

  • One button system: pill buttons with the offset 1/2/2/1 border, Oswald 700, three sizes (56, 48 and 44px).
  • WCAG AA contrast with kit colors only, 44px tap targets, and menus that work by keyboard.
  • Sponsor a Child stays the main goal, designed mobile first.
  • Every round tests three ways to organize the menu, so the navigation choice comes with evidence.

Changed in round 2

  • The brief now follows the 2026 board reports: partnerships, the next generation of supporters, and ongoing relationships.
  • Pathways to Purpose leads the hero in a seasonal slot that can change with each campaign.
  • A full navigation overhaul with a mega menu, as directors have requested.
  • Dignity, Health, Joy and Love appear together. Round 1's pillar to program mapping was retired.
  • Confirmed facts: 8 countries, 1,397 wells, a ministry that began in 1973, and the Eggum Family Medical Center.

Before any build

Open items

  • Sponsorship price. Round 3 uses the Pathways to Purpose price of $132 a month, about $5 a day (October 21). Rounds 1 and 2 show $93. The live sponsorship page still showed $93 on October 29 and should match before launch.
  • Brian photos. Approved and now in the round 3 stories rail. Rounds 1 and 2 still show placeholders.
  • Next Walk 4 Water. The October 25 Orange County walk has passed and the live homepage lists no upcoming walk. Round 3 holds a slot for the next date from the Events list.
  • Water 4 Kids. The Water 4 Kids International Director to confirm what $10,500 covers, and whether to mention well inspections.
  • Where Most Needed. A one-line description of the fund for direction C.
  • Graduate story. Pick one Brandom Vocational testimony video for direction B.
  • Partner logos. Space is held until logos and permissions arrive.
  • Nepal flood relief. Temporary slot; remove when the appeal ends.
  • Kids in program. Count on hold for review.
  • TikTok. @hope4kidsint exists but is not linked from the current site. Link it in the new footer?
  • Sign in. A2 offers two sign ins: the Givecloud Giving account and the Sponsor portal for letters. Decide whether they stay separate, and confirm the Giving account line matches what the Givecloud portal shows.
  • Tax line. "Gifts are tax-deductible" sits under the hero buttons. Tax language for supporters goes to H4KI leadership review before launch.
