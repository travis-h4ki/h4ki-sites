Giving Guide page wireframes

Three layout directions for the web version of the 2026 printed Giving Guide. Every direction carries all 21 guide gifts with the guide's prices and page numbers, and every give button opens that gift's live Givecloud giving experience. Grayscale on purpose: this round is about structure, not visual design.

A

Gift Catalog

Closest to today's page. Most requested gifts up top, then a filterable card grid of every gift by category.

Best for: browsing and fast giving. Easiest Elementor build (Loop Grid or tabs). B

The Guide, Page by Page

Mirrors the printed guide: Tom's thank you, the table of contents, then one chapter per printed spread in print order.

Best for: supporters arriving from the mailed guide or a QR code. C

Gift Finder

Budget first. Pick an amount range and see every gift at that price, plus a lane for shared big gifts and monthly giving.

Best for: "I have $50, what can I give?" visitors and mobile.

Recommendation: build A, and borrow C's "Share a bigger gift" box for the well, safe home, and goals. A maps cleanly to native Elementor widgets and keeps the page short; page numbers on every card cover the print readers B is designed for.

Fix before launch

Things the wireframes surfaced in Givecloud. These need a decision from finance or a form edit, not a page change.

Two gifts have no form

Bedding ($15, fund 41102) and Fruit Trees ($60, fund 42031) are in the guide but have no active Givecloud experience. Their buttons read "Form needed" in the wireframes.

Two fund codes differ from print

Feed a Family in Mexico: guide prints 91201, the Mexico Food Boxes form posts to 91200. Mattresses: guide prints 41118, the Sleeping Kits form posts to 11118. One side of each is wrong.

Form presets don't match guide prices

14 of the 20 linked forms use Givecloud's automatic amounts ($65, $125, $225, $500, $1,250, default $150). A supporter choosing a $10 Bible or a $50 Christmas gift lands on a form preset to $150.

Sewing machine price

The guide says $283. The form's custom presets ($60, $119, $238) look built around $238. Confirm which is right.

Feed a Child: two forms, one fund

The guide QR opens Food 4 Kids Team (monthly headline). One-time gifts ($60, $300, $600) are wired to Child Feeding Program here. Both post to 11212. Pick one, or keep the split.

Goals aren't set in Givecloud

Every form carries the default $10,000 goal. The guide's goals ($5,000 Health & Wellness, $3,000 coats, $5,800 sports, 100 Mexico families) would need to be entered before any progress bar can be shown honestly.

Good news since the Sep 23 audit: the Goats form now posts to 41103, matching the guide. Welding Kits and Sewing Machine still share 41010 with BVSC Student Sponsorship, as printed.
