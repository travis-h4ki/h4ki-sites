02 · Message from Leadership

02  ·  Message from Leadership

Angie Simon

Executive Director

This is my first year leading Hope 4 Kids International as Executive Director, after twenty years of serving alongside this team. We set out to grow our partnerships and to build for the long term, especially in Child Sponsorship and in operating support for the Eggum Family Medical Center.

On April 10 and 11 the medical center opened its doors to the community. In August, partners gave close to $1 million, the highest giving month in our history, including the largest single day of giving we have ever seen. In September, a team traveled to Cambodia for the first time, together with our Philippines trip.

This has also been a season of sorrow. Flooding brought tremendous loss to Nepal, a country we have come to love deeply. Our church partners in Mexico walked through a heartbreaking tragedy, and we stand with them in prayer and support. Pastor Neri Francisco of Ministerios Belen, our longtime partner in Guatemala, passed away after a period of declining health, and we honor him as one of our Heroes of the Faith. Ebola closed Uganda to our summer teams; Uganda is now Ebola free, and the November team is going.

Our partnerships are about so much more than buildings. They are relationships built over many years. We have much to be thankful for, much to look forward to, and many more lives to impact together.

Angie Simon

A message from our founder

Tom Eggum

Founder and President

Going into 2026, I predicted it would be our best year yet. On August 22 you celebrated my 75th birthday with our first ever Founder's Day, and together you gave about $66,000 for the Eggum Family Medical Center. I am humbled and grateful.

Training ministers was part of our mission from the beginning, and this year it moved forward again. We partnered with TLI on pastor training, and the Ministry Training Center is now under construction in Uganda.

I shared our story at four speaking engagements this spring, including preaching at my home church. Thank you for being part of this story.

Tom Eggum

06  ·  Mission Teams · Amy Bista

Mission Teams

Serving together

Trips filled so fast we kept a waitlist, and when Ebola halted Uganda travel, we waited together.

A year of full trips

Our May Guatemala and July Uganda trips filled so fast that we started a waitlist. Summer teams to Romania and Guatemala went well, we sent our first team with a church youth group, and a July Vacation Bible School team served in Mexico. In September we sent our first team to Cambodia, combined with the Philippines trip.

When we could not go

In June, after the U.S. State Department raised Uganda's travel advisory to Level 4 during an Ebola outbreak, we and our partners decided together to postpone the Orange Lutheran High School trip. We have run Uganda trips through earlier outbreaks; this one called for a different response. The July Uganda trip was canceled because of Ebola and insurance limits. Uganda is now Ebola free, and the November team is set to go.

Every team member, an Ambassador

We now invite each team member to carry an envelope with three children who are waiting for sponsors. Our goal is 100 children sponsored each year by the friends and family of the people who travel with us. Pharmacy students from the Guatemala trip are turning their experience into a research project, and Nina joined our Teams staff in January.

Child Sponsorship

Partners fully covered the urgent medical needs of 17 children this summer, in just two months.

Health first

Every student at LPA received a preventive health checkup this spring, and follow up care is underway. Except for a handful of LPA students still pending, partners funded every child with an urgent medical need. In June and July alone, $6,301 covered 17 children.

Caring for the people who care

Staff who work directly with children in Uganda received trauma and abuse training through our partnership with Mending the Soul. The Uganda team is already interviewing and enrolling next year's baby class at three primary schools, and both new partners in Cambodia are moving forward with sponsorship.

Every student passed

Every LPA student passed the P7 exam and every Fairmont student passed the S4 exam. Constant Emokor joined us as Education Manager in Uganda.

Brandom Vocational Technical Institute

87 students graduated in July. A new Automotive Mechanics program is fully funded, and a consultant is building the plan.

Watson Primary School

The new four classroom block at Watson Primary School is complete. Through Adopt A Village, the school is preparing to break ground on three latrines, pavers, and a library and administration block, while its coffee and banana crops are thriving and help support the school.

Across the villages

At Kasolo, the Musana Primary School dining shade is complete and a three classroom block is under construction. At Nyafumba we are designing a boarding section for P6 and P7. In Nepal, the second phase of Saraswati Basic School was dedicated in March and the third phase was funded in April. The Lee Family Foundation committed to build two dorms at LPA.

Water is Life

Clean, safe water

In Nepal this March, one of 150 filter buckets changes hands.

Wells

Large scale projects

The campus wastewater and soak pit project is complete. Water for Tororo Girls Secondary School, home to 2,300 boarding students, is underway, and it earns our sponsored girls half tuition. 225 filtration systems traveled with our teams this year: 150 to Nepal and 75 to Guatemala.

13  ·  Global Engagement · Jenny Herzog

Global Engagement

Carrying the story

Twenty five new Ambassadors joined this year, carrying our story to campuses, churches and companies.

Ambassadors

We relaunched the Ambassador program with missions that spread the word about key campaigns, plus a Difference Maker Kit. 61 former team members answered our Ambassador survey, and 25 new Ambassadors joined.

Companies and campuses

We built a tiered Corporate Sponsorship program for vocational training, the medical center, Food 4 Kids and Water 4 Kids, with flyers, video testimonials and a giving menu. Campus partnerships with Arizona State University, Grand Canyon University, St. John Fisher and Scholars Academy are shaping a 2027 Uganda trip designed for college students.

Churches and community

A presentation at St. Matthew's Lutheran Church in Virginia raised $7,750 for Namibia. A supporter led drive collected 1,500+ essential items and sponsored 6 girls, and a national women's Christian magazine published a team member's story of finding purpose in Uganda.
